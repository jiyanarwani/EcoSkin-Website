Natural Skincare Recipes For Dry Skin

Explore easy and effective remedies for glowing, healthy skin.

1. Whip Up an Olive Oil Moisturizer

Olive oil is packed with Vitamin E and antioxidants that repair dry skin. Note: It may not be suitable for acne-prone skin.

  • Use pure extra-virgin olive oil.
  • Apply sparingly to dry patches or mix into face masks.
  • Consult a dermatologist before use.

2. DIY Avocado Face Mask

Avocado is loaded with antioxidants that soothe and nourish skin.

  • ½ Avocado
  • ¼ cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tsp turmeric & drizzle of manuka honey
  • Apply for 10 minutes and rinse off.

3. Coconut Oil & Sugar Scrub

Exfoliate dead skin cells for smoother, softer skin.

  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • ½ cup coconut oil
  • Optional: lavender essential oil
  • Gently scrub skin and rinse with lukewarm water.

4. Oatmeal Bath Soak

Oatmeal soothes dry, irritated skin and locks in moisture.

  • 1 cup of oatmeal
  • Add to warm bath water
  • Optional: Tie oats in pantyhose and run water through them for a mess-free soak.

5. Oatmeal & Honey Exfoliator

A gentle exfoliator that hydrates and soothes dry skin.

  • 2 tbsp oatmeal
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • Dash of water
  • Use as a scrub or leave on for 20 minutes as a mask.
